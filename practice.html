The practice

Analyze. Review. Align.

We analyze, review, and align your products and processes so the flow is smoother, without adding headcount. Coalesce Ops examines the work as it is done, then leaves a written account of what is split and what must join.

What “coalesce” means here

It does not mean a new slogan, a reorganization chart, or a tool that replaces the ones you already run. It means the separate pieces of an operation agree on the work, the owner, and the week.

The review covers the people who move the work, the systems that record it, the vendors and tools the work depends on, and the leadership that owns the decisions. Security and compliance sit in that same account: the evidence has to match the controls you actually run. A gap in any one of those shows up in the others.

You receive a direct account: what is already sound, what is split, and the sequence in which to join it. The first visit is scoped in writing before it is scheduled. Work that follows is agreed separately.

Taking advantage of AI is part of that work. We put it on steps people already do, review the exposure and the cost, and align it with the flow. It does not arrive as a new team.

In scope

IT strategy and governance; security and compliance, including SOC 2 and PCI, with evidence that matches the controls in use; systems, identity, and access after a merger or acquisition; SaaS governance and vendor cost; AI used on the work, not beside it; automation of onboarding, offboarding, access review, and that evidence; the identity, network, and platforms the operation already runs; and the people who keep it, with one account for the executives who own it.
