Services

Operations, security, and infrastructure.

Operations, security, and infrastructure are examined together. Most engagements use more than one of these, because a gap in one place is usually visible in the others.

  1. 01

    IT operations

    Stable platforms, maintained runbooks, change control that is observed, and monitoring directed to the responsible owner.

    Platforms · Runbooks · Change control · Monitoring

  2. 02

    Security operations

    Detection and response that connect the security operations center, information technology, and leadership, so alerts result in decisions.

    Detection · Response · The security operations center · Leadership

  3. 03

    Security architecture

    Identity, network, cloud, and endpoint controls designed as one posture.

    Identity · Network · Cloud · Endpoint

  4. 04

    ISO readiness

    ISO/IEC 27001 and related control work treated as an operating requirement: evidence that can be produced, owners who understand their duties, and gaps addressed before assessment.

    ISO/IEC 27001 · Evidence · Owners · Gaps before assessment

  5. 05

    Penetration testing

    Testing informed by adversary methods, focused on the paths that matter, followed by remediation until findings are closed and retested.

    Adversary methods · Paths that matter · Remediation · Retest

  6. 06

    Infrastructure

    On-premises, hybrid, and cloud foundations with defined capacity, resilience, backup, and recovery.

    On-premises · Hybrid · Cloud · Backup and recovery
