Where the work splits

A rough flow often looks like a headcount problem. It is usually products and processes that do not agree, so good people spend the week bridging them. A handoff has no owner. Two systems disagree about the same job. A second brand still runs its own week.

Leadership then hears a different account than the people doing the work. A control written for an audit can describe a different operation than the one on the floor. Coalesce Ops watches how the work actually moves, including security, the vendor stack, and spend, and writes what must join for the operation to run as one.

In scope: IT strategy and governance, security and compliance including SOC 2 and PCI, SaaS and what it costs, identity and access, automation of onboarding, offboarding, and the evidence, and one account for the executives who own the operation.

Taking advantage of AI

AI pays when it carries steps your people already do. We analyze which of those steps it can take, review the exposure and the cost, and align it with the products and processes in the flow.

The gain is smoother work. The constraint is the same one: no added headcount to watch the model. A tool that needs its own team is not an advantage.
